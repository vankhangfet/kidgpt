The reasoning engine · Instead of one question with three answers, the child investigates. Sparkle walks through the contradiction step by step, gives a hint on a wrong choice, and only lets the child accuse after the logic is clear. Case files continue into the Blue Backpack (reading) and the Secret Code (math: 5+3, 12−7, 2×4 → 8-5-8).
